Our short answer

Abroady does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage, which works like a cookie, only to make the app work: to keep you logged in, remember your language and save your progress.

Third-party services

Google Fonts is optional. If you choose "Accept all", the site loads the Inter font from Google, which receives your IP address. If you choose "Essential only", we use your device's built-in fonts and Google is not contacted.

Formspree is only contacted when you press a button to send a form. It does not place cookies through our site.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page, or remove everything by clearing your browser's site data for Abroady.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
