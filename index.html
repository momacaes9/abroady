👋 Meet the founder

Monica Macaes

First-year at UNC-Chapel Hill from São Paulo, Brazil, studying Economics and Data Science. I'm building Abroady because I needed it on my first day.

When I arrived as an international student, the information I needed was spread across dozens of websites, and study abroad felt like something only juniors and seniors did. I wanted one clear path, and people on the same timeline as me.

Before Abroady, I co-founded One Step Drives All, an environmental initiative in Brazil, where I built AI apps and websites and analyzed 300+ surveys. I tutor math to students in 15+ countries, and I've pitched ideas to international panels as a global finalist in Pitch for the Future and a Top 16 finalist in the Global Youth Challenge.

  • 🌎 Portuguese, English, Italian, Spanish
  • 💻 Python, SQL, HTML/CSS/JavaScript
  • 🎓 UNC-Chapel Hill, Class of 2030
