Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Who we are

Abroady is an early-stage student project founded by Monica Macaes, an undergraduate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Abroady is independent and is not operated, affiliated with or endorsed by UNC-Chapel Hill, UNSW Sydney or any other university. In this policy, "Abroady", "we" and "us" mean the founder of Abroady.

Contact for any privacy question or request: momacaes@unc.edu.

2. What we collect

Information you send us through forms

When you join the early-access list, sign up for event updates, send a message or leave a review, we receive what you type into that form. Depending on the form this is your name, email address, university, year of study, dream destination, whether you are an international student, your message, and your review and star rating. We also receive the language you were using and the date and time you sent it.

Account information (prototype)

During the prototype, accounts are created and stored only in your own browser. Your password never leaves your device: we store a salted, one-way hash of it in your browser, never the password itself. When you create an account, we receive a notification with your name, email, home country, year, term abroad and international-student status, so we can contact you about the beta. We do not receive your password.

Progress saved on your device

Your checklist progress, the clubs you join, your language and your privacy choices are saved in your browser's local storage so the app remembers them. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

3. How we use your information

  • To send you the early-access invite, event updates or replies you asked for.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To understand what students need and improve Abroady.
  • To publish your review on the site, only if you agreed to publication, showing your first name and the description you gave.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Legal bases (GDPR and Brazil's LGPD)

We process form data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the box on each form. We process technical data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a secure website. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us; this does not affect processing that already happened.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Formspree (United States) receives form submissions and delivers them to our email.
  • Netlify (United States) hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC, United States) supplies the website's font, only if you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner. Loading a font shares your IP address with Google.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never share your information with universities, employers or other students without your permission.

6. International transfers

Abroady and its providers are based in the United States. If you use Abroady from another country, including Brazil or a country in the European Union, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

7. How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for up to 24 months after your last contact with us, or until you ask us to delete them. Data saved in your browser stays there until you delete your account in the app, clear your browser data, or log out and remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of Brazil have these rights under the LGPD, residents of the EU and UK under the GDPR, and California residents under the CCPA. To use any of these rights, email momacaes@unc.edu. We will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority, such as Brazil's ANPD.

You can delete the data stored in your browser at any time under My account → Delete my account and data.

9. Age

Abroady is meant for university students. Do not use Abroady or send us information if you are under 16. If we learn that we received information from someone under 16, we will delete it.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, passwords are stored only as salted hashes on your device, and we limit the information we collect to what we need. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as passport or visa numbers through our forms.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and ask for your agreement again through the banner on the site.
